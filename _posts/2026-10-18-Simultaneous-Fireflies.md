After making code for the Big Dipper, Flash Bulb, and Blue Ghost fireflies, it is now time to combine them all in one!
This code will allow us to see the three behaviors simultaneously on our LilyPad USB.

To understand what's going on with the LEDs:
Pin A7 behaves like Big Dipper
Pin A5 behaves like the Flash Bulb
Pin A8 behaves like Blue Ghost
