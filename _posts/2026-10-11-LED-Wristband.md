Here we were tasked to make a light-up LED wristband that uses two switches where one is a homemade switch and the other is a conductive snap. My homemade switch consists paper clip that when it is taken off, one of the lights disconnects. My wristband also takes inspiration of the red omnitrix from the kid TV-show Ben-10 thought it would be cool to bring the nostalgia back. Also a tip/trick that I used while sewing the components together is to make sure that the non-conductive threads are tight when you're taking  them and also make sure to secure the components again tightly with the conductive thread. I hope you all like my wristband :)
