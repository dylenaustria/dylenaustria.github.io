Here it is guys! Time for a new project! It has been a while since we've done a project so here is what I plan to do:

This past Halloween, I dressed up as Sharkboy from the kid movie Sharkboy and Lavagirl. So for this project, my goal is to bring back the nostalgia and redesign my costume so that the shark jaw on the shirts/vest lights up and have sunglasses that will light up as well. With our Lilypad components, I plan to incorporate a buzzer and a light sensor on the shirt so that when the Lilypad is turned on, the buzzer will play the Dream song from the movie. With the light sensor, I will have it coded so that when it is dark out, the LED lights will increase the brightness. Additionally, I will have the LED lights in sync with the beat from the Dream song.
