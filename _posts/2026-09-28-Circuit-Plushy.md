Here we were tasked to make a light-up plushy that uses a slide switch and a button to light on/off described in Q5 of the Simple Circuits Worksheet. I connected the electronic components with conductive thread. After connecting the components together, I decorated with embroidery floss using a couple of stitches and finally assembled the plushy with non-conductive thread. I hope you all like my plushy :)
